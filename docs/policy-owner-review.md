## Privacy notice draft

Zayan Ahmed Travels Private Limited operates www.zayanahmedtravels.com. Questions about information handled through the website and travel desk can be directed to ceo@zayanahmedtravels.com or Pine View Road, Main Cantt Chowk, Abbottabad. The owner should confirm the contact route for formal privacy requests.

The website receives contact and trip information through inquiries and supports customer registration, sign-in, password recovery, and verified linked-booking access. Staff records can contain customer and passenger details, itineraries, booking references, travel-document fields, payments, receipts, and service notes. The final notice needs an approved list of information actually collected for each service and the purpose of each item.

Accounts use password hashes. Customer sign-in uses the customer_session cookie with a seven-day expiry in the current implementation. Staff session settings require a separate final inventory. Recovery links are time-limited and single-use, and resetting a password revokes existing customer sessions.

The intended purposes are responding to requests, administering agreed travel services, maintaining accounts and booking records, recording payments, and protecting the service. The final notice needs the business's approved purposes, consent or other applicable basis, supplier disclosures, and any marketing practices.

The application is hosted through Hostinger and its application database uses Supabase PostgreSQL. Account recovery email uses Hostinger SMTP. Encrypted application-schema recovery copies are retained on the owner's Windows system, and an isolated local restore has been verified. Document-upload activation and complete object recovery remain under review. Actual processor locations, supplier recipients, offsite backup practices, and safeguards require owner confirmation.

The final notice needs an approved retention and deletion schedule covering inquiries, accounts, traveler documents, financial records, audit logs, and recovery copies. Requests for access or correction should go through the approved contact and identity-verification process. Applicable rights, response handling, and any legal exceptions require review before publication.

## Website and travel service terms draft

The website is operated by Zayan Ahmed Travels Private Limited. Contact the travel desk at ceo@zayanahmedtravels.com or Pine View Road, Main Cantt Chowk, Abbottabad.

Use the website to request assistance and review the bookings linked to your verified account. Before agreeing to a service, check the written traveler details, itinerary, supplier confirmations, inclusions, exclusions, price, currency, and applicable conditions with the travel desk.

Provide accurate traveler and contact information and promptly report errors. The travel desk should confirm the requirements and deadlines applicable to the requested service. Visa, entry, permit, and supplier decisions remain with the relevant authorities and providers.

The accepted written quotation or confirmation should state price, currency, validity, payment arrangements, and service-specific terms. Use payment instructions confirmed by the business and retain the receipt for payments made.

Obtain the applicable change and cancellation conditions before accepting a service. The approved service schedule should explain fees, deadlines, supplier conditions, refund assessment, and payout arrangements.

Keep account credentials and recovery links private. Contact the travel desk about suspected account access or booking errors. Verified account ownership is required before linked bookings become visible.

The owner must finalize the booking-acceptance process, service obligations, supplier disclosures, complaints process, applicable jurisdiction and dispute terms, and effective date before publishing these terms.

## Refund and cancellation information draft

Send change or cancellation requests to ceo@zayanahmedtravels.com with the booking reference, traveler details, and requested change. The business should confirm who may request a change on another traveler's behalf and what identity verification is needed.

Each request should be reviewed against the accepted booking terms, supplier conditions, services already provided, and payments recorded. The final policy needs a separate approved schedule for flights, hotels, Umrah, tours, transport, and visas.

The schedule should explain eligibility, no-show and partial-use treatment, change or reissue costs, supplier charges, refund approval, processing estimates, currency and tax treatment, and complaint escalation. It should also identify supplier recovery dependencies and the payout method and payee-verification process.

Refund approval, credit creation, and payment of money should be recorded and reconciled as separate events. The customer should receive confirmation of the approved outcome and of any actual payout.
